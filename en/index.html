How to recognise good tile work

Four details that make the difference.

These four points decide whether a surface looks like craft or like a DIY store. Ask about them on every quote you get – ours and our competitors’.

Mitre instead of trim

On external corners we cut both slabs to a mitre instead of fitting an aluminium trim. More work, but the edge disappears.

A planned slab layout

The setting-out plan comes before the first cut: where the axis sits, where the cut lands, how the joint runs around the niche. No slivers in the line of sight.

Continuous joint lines

Wall and floor joints meet. It sounds obvious, but you will not see it in most bathrooms – because it takes planning before anything is bought.

Waterproofing that lasts

Bonded waterproofing to standard, sealing tape in every corner, a fall towards the drain. Nobody sees it later – which is exactly why it is where most corners get cut.

Maintenance & care

A bathroom gets finished. A joint never does.

Silicone ages, joints crack, and the most expensive water damage starts as a hairline crack nobody notices. We come by once a year and keep the bathroom sealed – included for five years on Signature projects.

  • Annual bathroom inspectionJoints, silicone, connections and falls checked
  • Silicone servicingOld joints out, new ones in – before anything moves behind them
  • Natural stone careRefreshing the sealer, removing limescale and stains properly
  • Replacing individual tilesWe keep spare material aside – it still matches years later
