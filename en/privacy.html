6. Contact form and getting in touch

The enquiry form on this website does not transmit any data to our server. When you submit it, your local email application opens with a prepared message; your details only reach us once you send that message yourself, in the same way as an ordinary email.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where the contact relates to entering into or performing a contract (for example preparing a quote), otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. We delete the data once it is no longer needed and no statutory retention period applies.
