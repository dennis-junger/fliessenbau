Contractual basis

Terms and Conditions

The German version is the legally binding one. This English text is a convenience translation of the AGB and should be checked by a lawyer before use. Invalid clauses simply fall away, and statutory law then applies – often to your disadvantage.

§ 1 Scope

These terms apply to all contracts for tiling, natural stone, mosaic and drywall work and for the procurement of material between us and the client. Differing terms of the client only become effective if we agree to them in writing.

Towards consumers these terms only apply where they have been validly incorporated and no statutory provision takes precedence. Statutory consumer rights, in particular the right of withdrawal, remain unaffected.

§ 2 Quotation and conclusion of contract

Our quotations are without obligation unless expressly described as binding. The basis of the quotation is the site measurement carried out by us. The contract comes into effect with our written order confirmation or when work begins.

On-site measurement and initial consultation are free of charge unless the quotation states otherwise. Costs for a sample area or a 3D visualisation are shown separately in advance.

§ 3 Material and samples

Natural stone, hand-made tiles such as zellige and historic cement tiles are natural products or handmade goods. Variations in colour, veining, glaze, dimensions and surface compared with samples and images are typical of the material and do not constitute a defect.

With imported goods, delivery times may shift due to circumstances beyond our control. We will inform you without delay and agree a new date.

Material supplied by the client is laid after inspection for obvious defects. We accept no liability for the suitability, the quantity calculation or hidden defects of such material.

§ 4 The client's obligation to cooperate

The client ensures that the site is accessible and cleared on the agreed date and that electricity and water are available, and informs us of concealed pipes and cables.

Delays resulting from a lack of cooperation or from preliminary work by other trades extend the agreed deadlines appropriately. Additional costs arising from this, for example a wasted journey, may be charged separately.

§ 5 Execution, deadlines and acceptance

Agreed dates are binding once confirmed in writing. The agreed surface or finishing quality follows from the quotation.

On completion we inspect the work together and record the result in an acceptance report. If the client puts the work into use without declaring acceptance, it is deemed accepted after twelve working days unless material defects have been raised.

§ 6 Prices and payment

The prices stated in the quotation apply plus statutory VAT. Invoicing is based on the actual measurement unless a fixed price has been agreed.

Invoices are payable within 14 days of receipt without deduction. For longer projects, instalments based on progress may be agreed. We only require payment in advance for material on special orders and state this in the quotation.

§ 7 Defects

Statutory rights in respect of defects apply. The limitation period for work on a building is five years from acceptance.

Defects must be reported to us without delay after discovery and we must be given the opportunity to remedy them. We are not liable for damage caused by improper care, unsuitable cleaning products or moisture originating from the building. Care instructions are handed over at acceptance.

§ 8 Liability

We are liable without limitation in cases of intent and gross negligence and for injury to life, body or health. In cases of simple negligence we are liable only for breach of a material contractual obligation and limited in amount to the foreseeable damage typical of the contract.

§ 9 Retention of title

Material supplied remains our property until paid for in full, unless it has become an integral part of the building through installation.

§ 10 Final provisions

The law of the Federal Republic of Germany applies. If the client is a merchant, a legal entity under public law or a special fund under public law, the place of jurisdiction is Berlin.

Should any provision be invalid, the validity of the remaining provisions is unaffected. The statutory provision takes the place of the invalid one.

We are neither willing nor obliged to take part in dispute resolution proceedings before a consumer arbitration board.
